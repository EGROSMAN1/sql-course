⚡ Create a Calculated Column

📚 Examples (row-wise operation!):
Profit = player_stats[PTS] - player_stats[TOV]
FullName = player_stats[player] & " (" & player_stats[team] & ")"
Grade = IF(player_stats[PTS] >= 25, "Star", "Regular")
Performance = SWITCH(TRUE(), player_stats[PTS]>=30, "Elite", player_stats[PTS]>=20, "Star", "Bench")
Index = RANKX(ALL(player_stats), player_stats[PTS])

📊 Create a calculated table (Calculated Table)

📚 Examples:
Top 10 = TOPN(10, player_stats, player_stats[PTS])
Summary = SUMMARIZE(player_stats, player_stats[team], "Avg PTS", AVERAGE(player_stats[PTS]))
Calendar = CALENDAR(DATE(2024,1,1), DATE(2024,12,31))
Numbers = GENERATESERIES(1, 100, 1)
Filtered = FILTER(player_stats, player_stats[PTS] > 25)
Combined = UNION(player_stats, team_stats)
Joined = NATURALLEFTOUTERJOIN(player_stats, team_stats)
