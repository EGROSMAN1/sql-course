The situation

You joined the business research department atA large insurance company. Management is struggling to make decisions about The management fees paid for the savings products of employees at specific employers. You've been asked to investigate the data and provide business insights.

Management fee = the commission paid to the insurance company from the saver's account. There are two types: Management fee from deposit (percentage of the monthly deposit) andManagement fee from accumulation (percentage of the total balance). The two sums in percentages — if it says "2" it means 2%.
