ABOUT · A bit about me

Hi, I'm Eliran Grossman, a data analyst and analytics team lead at Partner Communications. For years I’ve seen how hard it is for people here to break into the data field — most materials are in English, courses are expensive, and the jump to job interviews is confusing.

My goal is simple — to make the world of data accessible to as many people as possible, for free.

This site is exactly that: one place that brings together everything you need to learn SQL, Python, and Pandas from the basics to interview level, with real-world examples in language everyone understands.
